Project Overview

A Data-Driven Framework for Dynamic Stratification and Decision Modeling

The project transforms raw hospital records into organized, searchable and visual information. It combines historical data retrieval, AI-based analysis, risk stratification and follow-up workflows to help users understand patient and hospital data more clearly.

Concept

What the Project Does

The system takes raw hospital information and converts it into structured data, meaningful analysis and understandable visual information.

01

Data Collection

Collects patient records, hospital visits, diseases, admissions, procedures, laboratory information and expenditure.

02

Data Organization

Cleans and organizes raw records so that information can be searched and analyzed consistently.

03

Patient Search

Allows users to search for a patient and view their available history, visits, risk and expenditure.

04

Context Retrieval

Retrieves relevant historical information when a user's question or analysis requires additional patient context.

05

Risk Stratification

Groups cases into low, medium and high-risk categories according to the project's predictive analysis.

06

Follow-up

High-priority cases can be identified for appropriate follow-up and re-evaluation when new information becomes available.

The final results are presented through the dashboard and can be used for controlled follow-up workflows.

Final Understanding

What Makes the Project Useful

The project takes raw hospital records and transforms them into structured and understandable information. Users can search individual patients, view their historical records, analyze hospital visits and expenditure, understand disease patterns, and visualize the information through dashboards and charts. RAG retrieves relevant historical information when required, while AI helps analyze and summarize the retrieved context. The system then performs risk stratification, explains the important factors behind the result, identifies cases requiring follow-up, and tracks changes when new records are added. This creates a continuous flow from raw data to analysis, visualization, prioritization and re-evaluation.

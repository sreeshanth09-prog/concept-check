IEEE Conference Research Concept

A Contextual-Temporal Intelligence Framework for Adaptive Resource Orchestration and Personalized Decision Synthesis

A research-oriented intelligent framework that treats personalized food planning as a dynamic resource-allocation problem under economic, nutritional, behavioral, temporal and operational constraints.

01 — Project Overview

Concept

The system combines nutritional intelligence, economic optimization and adaptive planning to create practical food plans for users with limited and fixed budgets.

The central idea is to determine what a person can realistically purchase, prepare and consume within a fixed budget while attempting to satisfy nutritional requirements. The system continuously adapts the recommendation when food prices, available inventory, leftovers, cooking constraints or user behavior change.

02 — Problem Definition

What Problem Does It Solve?

Existing food-planning approaches often treat nutrition, cost and food availability separately. This project attempts to solve them together.

01

Limited Food Budget

Users may have a fixed weekly or monthly amount available for food and still need an appropriate nutritional plan.

02

Changing Grocery Prices

Food prices change over time, which can make a previously generated plan exceed the available budget.

03

Conflicting Requirements

Budget, nutrition, dietary restrictions, cooking time and food preferences can conflict with one another.

04

Food Waste

Ingredients may remain unused, expire or be purchased in quantities that do not match actual consumption.

05

Inefficient Spending

The cheapest food is not always the most useful food when nutritional value and total meal requirements are considered.

06

Static Meal Planning

A fixed plan does not normally react to price changes, inventory changes, missed meals or actual user behavior.

04 — System Workflow

End-to-End Workflow

The system follows a closed-loop process rather than generating a one-time diet plan.

STEP 01

User Context

Goal, budget, diet, activity, preferences, kitchen, cooking time and household size.

STEP 02

Nutrition Analysis

Estimate nutritional requirements and target ranges.

STEP 03

Economic Analysis

Determine whether the nutritional objective is financially feasible.

STEP 04

Price Integration

Incorporate current and historical grocery prices.

STEP 05

Optimization

Find the best food quantities under multiple constraints.

STEP 06

Grocery Basket

Convert optimized quantities into a practical shopping list.

STEP 07

Meal Allocation

Distribute ingredients across meals and days.

STEP 08

Recipe Generation

Generate cooking instructions from the selected ingredients.

STEP 09

Consumption

User records meals, purchases and remaining ingredients.

STEP 10

Inventory

Track available food, leftovers and estimated expiry.

STEP 11

Change Detection

Detect meaningful price, inventory or behavioral changes.

STEP 12

Re-Optimization

Adapt the plan while preserving the most important goals.

07 — Methodology

Proposed Method

The system combines deterministic mathematical optimization with AI-based natural-language generation.

01

Context Acquisition

Collect goal, budget, diet type, activity level, food preferences, cooking time, kitchen equipment and household requirements.

02

Nutritional Requirement Estimation

Estimate target energy and nutritional requirements using the user's supplied profile and selected goal.

03

Economic Feasibility Analysis

Determine whether the desired nutritional target can be achieved under the available monetary constraint.

04

Candidate Food Selection

Filter candidate foods according to diet, preferences, price, nutritional properties, shelf life and kitchen requirements.

05

Multi-Objective Optimization

Optimize quantities using cost, nutrition, ingredient reuse, food waste, cooking time and user preferences.

06

Meal Allocation

Distribute optimized ingredients across daily meals while maintaining the required nutritional targets.

07

Generative Recipe Layer

Use the LLM to generate recipes and cooking instructions based on the already optimized ingredient set.

08

Inventory and Consumption Feedback

Track purchased quantities, consumed quantities, remaining ingredients and leftovers.

09

Temporal Price Analysis

Compare current prices with previous optimization states and identify significant economic changes.

10

Adaptive Re-Optimization

Recalculate affected decisions while attempting to preserve the user's highest-priority constraints.

12 — Experimental Design

Proposed Research Experiments

E1

Budget Optimization

Compare the proposed optimizer against a baseline meal-planning strategy under the same nutritional goals.

E2

Price Shock

Simulate 10%, 20%, 30% and 50% price changes and measure how effectively the system restores feasibility.

E3

Waste Reduction

Compare normal grocery planning with inventory-aware and leftover-aware planning.

E4

Constraint Conflicts

Introduce impossible combinations and evaluate whether the system identifies and resolves them.

E5

Household Planning

Compare independent meal plans with shared-meal optimization for multiple people.

E6

Energy-Aware Planning

Compare ingredient-only cost optimization against combined food and cooking-energy cost.

14 — Research Contribution

What Makes the Project Different?

Core Research Idea

Personalized food planning is formulated as a dynamic multi-objective resource-allocation problem where budget, nutrition, price variation, inventory, cooking limitations and user preferences are optimized together.

Adaptive Decision Layer

Instead of producing one static recommendation, the system continuously evaluates environmental and user-specific changes and selectively re-optimizes affected decisions.

Economic-Nutritional Trade-off

The system does not simply minimize food cost. It attempts to maximize nutritional adequacy and resource utilization while respecting monetary constraints.

Closed-Loop Planning

Purchases, consumption, leftovers and waste become feedback signals for subsequent planning cycles.
